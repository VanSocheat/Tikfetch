Frequently asked questions

Can I use TikFetch without an account?

Yes. The interface does not require registration.

Why aren't videos appearing yet?

This GitHub Pages version is frontend-only. A deployed backend and authorized provider must be connected before live video lookup works.

Can I download every public video?

No. Availability depends on permissions, provider capabilities, and applicable restrictions.

Does TikFetch support private profiles?

No. TikFetch does not bypass access controls.

Is TikFetch affiliated with TikTok?

No. TikFetch is an independent project and is not affiliated with TikTok.
